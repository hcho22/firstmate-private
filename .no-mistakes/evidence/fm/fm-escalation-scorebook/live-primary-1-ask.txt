⏺ Captain, my recommendation is absolute dates ("Nov 6").
  Right now the board shows the raw date on deferred items in Charted Next ("until 2026-11-06"), so either choice is a change from today.
  Why absolute:
  - The board doesn't update itself. It's built at one moment and often stays open for days, and the saved report gets re-read later. "In 3 days" is wrong by the next morning, but "Nov 6" stays
    correct.
  - It shows back the date you chose. When you push a decision to "later," you give a date, and the board would display that same date.
  - I'd use the short form and add the year only when the date isn't in the current year.
  The downside is that you have to work out how soon "Nov 6" is, while "in 3 days" tells you at a glance. If that becomes a nuisance, we could add a small relative hint for dates in the coming week. I
  don't think it's needed now.
  Which do you want, absolute or relative? Nothing gets built until you decide.
